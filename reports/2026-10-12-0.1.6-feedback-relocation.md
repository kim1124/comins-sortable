## Problem and correction

Human review observed a transient 3.695px vertical / 1.719px horizontal shift in `rotate(3deg) scale(0.9)` containers. A browser regression also reproduced 3.650px with scale alone. Position correction ran before moving the placeholder; removing the spacer changed the source list height and its percentage transform origin after that correction.

After placeholder placement and affected sorting animations are established, the Core now re-applies the same pointer position within the same frame. No portal, new API, dependency, or consumer-style change is introduced.
